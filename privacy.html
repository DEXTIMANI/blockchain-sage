Effective Date: February 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Blockchain Sage ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide gaming and mining simulation services, we collect:

  • Account Information: Name, Email Address, and User ID (via Firebase Authentication).
  • Financial Data: In-game balances and rewards (stored via Firebase Firestore).
  • Device Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (AAID) for ad delivery.
  • Usage Data: Game scores, interaction logs, and crash reports.

2. Third-Party Services

Our app integrates with third-party services that collect data to function:

  • Google Play Services: Used for app distribution and core functionality.
  • Firebase: Used for authentication, database hosting, and analytics.
  • AdMob: Used to serve advertisements within the app.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To manage your game account and track mining rewards.
  • To synchronize your game progress across devices.
  • To serve rewarded advertisements that allow for in-game boosts.
  • To protect our platform from fraud and "bot" activity.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data as long as your account is active. Users have the right to request the deletion of their account and all associated data.

To request data deletion, please visit: Account Deletion Request

5. Advertising and Analytics

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies and device identifiers to serve ads based on a user's prior visits. You can opt-out of personalized advertising by visiting your Android Device Settings.

6. Children’s Privacy

Blockchain Sage does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Our game is intended for users who have reached the age of majority in their jurisdiction.
